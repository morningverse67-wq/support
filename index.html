다임북 · ダイムブック
A book that stays. 남아 있는 책. 残る一冊.

Support

What am I supposed to write?
One line at a time, under the account it belongs to. Time, money, heart or people. An amount is optional, and a line without one still counts.
Where is my book kept?
On your device. With cloud backup on, a locked copy is also kept on our server. The key stays in your iCloud Keychain, so we cannot read it. Your member account is kept apart and isn't linked to the book.
My lines are gone.
If cloud backup was on, install the app again on an iPhone with the same iCloud Keychain and the book comes back. If it was off, deleting the app deleted the book with it.
The reminder never arrives.
Open Settings, then Notifications, then Dimebook, and allow notifications.
The widget is empty.
Open the app once after installing it, then remove and re-add the widget.

지원

무엇을 적나요?
한 번에 한 줄씩, 그 줄이 속한 계정 아래에 적습니다. 시간, 돈, 마음, 사람. 금액은 선택이고, 금액 없는 줄도 똑같이 한 줄입니다.
기록은 어디에 있나요?
기기 안에 있습니다. 클라우드 백업이 켜져 있으면 잠긴 사본이 저희 서버에도 있습니다. 여는 열쇠는 iCloud 키체인에만 있어서 저희는 읽을 수 없습니다. 회원 계정은 따로 있고 책과 이어지지 않습니다.
적은 줄이 사라졌어요.
클라우드 백업이 켜져 있었다면, 같은 iCloud 키체인을 쓰는 아이폰에 앱을 다시 설치하면 책이 돌아옵니다. 꺼져 있었다면 앱을 지울 때 책도 함께 지워졌습니다.
알림이 오지 않아요.
설정, 알림, 다임북 순서로 들어가 알림을 허용해 주세요.
위젯이 비어 있어요.
앱을 한 번 실행한 뒤 위젯을 지웠다가 다시 추가해 주세요.

サポート

何を書けばいいですか？
一度に一行ずつ、その行が属する勘定の下に書きます。時間、お金、心、人。金額は任意で、金額のない行も同じ一行です。
記録はどこに保存されますか？
端末内です。クラウドバックアップがオンなら、鍵をかけたコピーが当方のサーバーにもあります。鍵は iCloud キーチェーンにだけあるため、当方は読めません。会員アカウントは別にあり、帳簿とはつながりません。
書いた行が消えました。
クラウドバックアップがオンだった場合は、同じ iCloud キーチェーンの iPhone にアプリを再インストールすると帳簿が戻ります。オフだった場合は、アプリの削除とともに帳簿も消えています。
リマインダーが届きません。
設定、通知、ダイムブックの順に開き、通知を許可してください。
ウィジェットが空のままです。
アプリを一度開いてから、ウィジェットを削除して追加し直してください。

Privacy Policy · 개인정보 처리방침 · プライバシーポリシー

Effective 27 October 2026 · 시행일 2026년 10월 27일 · 施行日 2026年10月27日

Your book stays on your device, and the member list and the book are never linked.

To use Dimebook you sign in with Apple, Kakao or Google and become a member. Our member server holds only what it needs to recognise members (below), and no book. The cloud backup server holds only locked copies of books, with no member number or email. So we cannot read your book, or tell which copy belongs to which member. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party software development kits are included.

  • What we process, and why. Member data on the member server: sign-in method (Apple, Kakao or Google), the user identifier that provider gives us, your email address (only if the provider has verified it; with Apple's Hide My Email, the relay address Apple creates), a random member number, when you joined, when you last used the app, whether you agreed to Get news and when you changed it, and, if you signed in with Apple, the refresh token Apple issued (stored encrypted). Purposes: recognising you and keeping you signed in, giving member benefits (cloud backup and Reading), and disconnecting the sign-in provider when you delete your account; basis: performing the membership agreement (Korean Personal Information Protection Act, Art. 15(1)(4)). Get news (optional): only members who turn on Get news (optional) under Book › Member account receive news of new features and offers by email; it is off by default, can be turned off at any time, and the app shows the date of your choice; basis: consent (Art. 15(1)(1)). We do not store names, phone numbers, passwords or profile photos.
  • Cloud backup is off until you turn it on. The app locks your book file on your device (AES-GCM) and sends it to our backup server. The key stays in your iCloud Keychain and never reaches us. The server keeps only the locked file, a version number and the time it was last saved, filed under a random ID made from that key. Purpose: backing up the book and bringing it back on a device with the same iCloud Keychain.
  • How membership is shown without linking. When it backs up or fetches Reading, the app sends the sign-in token the member server signed. The backup server checks only its signature and expiry; it does not take out or store the member number in it. Reading articles come from the same server, and it does not record who read what.
  • IP addresses. Cloudflare handles the IP address each request comes from, to pass requests on and limit abuse. We do not store it, and neither server keeps request logs.
  • Email. If you email us: your address, any name you give, and the content and attachments, used only to reply. We create no pseudonymised data and use nothing for any other purpose.
  • Stored only on your device. Your book (accounts, lines, amounts, notes) and settings. Reminders are scheduled by iOS on your device and their text never leaves it. The sign-in token is kept only in this phone's keychain and does not move to another phone. Exporting produces a file that you choose where to send; the app has no part in what happens to it afterwards.
  • Payment. This version has no in-app purchase. Cloud backup and Reading are free for members to celebrate the launch. We never see card details.
  • Sharing, outsourcing and transfer abroad. We do not give personal data to third parties, and we do not hand the copies to anyone. Sign-in providers: when you sign in, Apple (Apple Inc., United States), Kakao (Kakao Corp., Korea) or Google (Google LLC, United States) gives us the result of checking who you are (user identifier and email address); what they share is asked on their sign-in screen and follows their privacy policies. When you delete your account, we ask Apple to revoke this app's sign-in token, or Kakao to unlink this app from your Kakao member number. Cloudflare: the member server, the backup server and their databases run on the cloud of Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare Workers and D1), which we entrust with running the servers and storing the data. Transferred: the member data above, locked copies of books, and IP addresses (not stored). Where: the databases are in a Cloudflare data centre in the Asia-Pacific region (Cloudflare decides which country in that region and does not disclose it); requests are handled by the Cloudflare data centre nearest to you; Cloudflare's head office is in the United States. When and how: over the internet (HTTPS) when you sign in, open the app, back up, fetch Reading, change Get news or delete your account. Recipient: Cloudflare, Inc., San Francisco, USA, privacy contact dpo@cloudflare.com. Purpose and period: running the servers, for the retention periods below. If you don't want this, don't sign up, or delete your account and turn cloud backup off; Dimebook can't be used without signing in. Email (support and news): we receive mail through Gmail by Google LLC, and also send news by Gmail to members who agreed to Get news. So your message (address, name, content, attachments) and the email addresses of members who receive news are stored by Google in the United States and other countries where it has data centres, from when the mail is sent until we delete it as below. Recipient: Google LLC, Mountain View, California, USA, privacy contact support.google.com/policies/contact/general_privacy_form (privacy policy policies.google.com/privacy). If you don't want this, don't email us and keep Get news off; without email we cannot answer you.
  • Retention and deletion. Member data is kept until you delete your account. Under Book › Member account, Delete member account deletes it from the member server at once, and we ask Apple or Kakao to disconnect too. The book stays on this phone. Deleted member data can remain in the database's recovery feature (Cloudflare D1 Time Travel) for up to 30 days and then disappears automatically; we keep no separate downloaded backup of member data. Deleting the app does not delete your member data, so delete your account first if you want to leave.
    The cloud copy stays until you turn cloud backup off; when you delete your account with cloud backup on, you can choose Delete with the cloud copy. You can turn cloud backup off under Book › Advanced. Turning it off deletes the copy on the server the next time the app is open and online. Turn it off on every device that uses the same iCloud Keychain; a device that still has it on uploads the copy again. Deleting the app does not delete the copy: it stays, and comes back if you install the app again with the same iCloud Keychain. To remove it, turn cloud backup off before deleting the app. If you no longer have the phone, install the app on another device with the same iCloud Keychain and turn it off there. A deleted copy stays in the server's recovery feature and the operator's once-a-day backup for up to 30 days, then is removed. With cloud backup off, deleting the app deletes your book along with it, so export first if you want to keep it.
    Emails are deleted from Gmail, including the trash, within one year after the matter is closed. Deletion means removing the rows from the database and the mail including the trash. No personal data is kept under any other law.
  • Your rights. Under Book › Member account you can see your sign-in method and email, turn off Get news, sign out and delete your account at any time. For member data and emails we hold, you, your legal representative or someone you authorise can ask to see, correct, delete or stop processing them by email; we reply within 10 days. We cannot tell which book copy is yours, so we cannot find it for you by email; turning cloud backup off is how you delete it.
  • Automatic collection. No cookies, advertising identifiers or analytics in the app, and no tracking. This support page is served by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visits (such as IP addresses) for security. We do not receive those logs.
  • Security. The book is locked on your device before it leaves, so we cannot read the copy. HTTPS only; sign-in tokens are signed by the member server and expire after 30 days without use; Apple refresh tokens are stored encrypted and server keys are kept only as server secrets. The statistics the operator sees show member counts only, no email addresses.
  • Children. Dimebook is not directed at children under 14. If we learn that member data belongs to a child under 14, we delete it at once.
